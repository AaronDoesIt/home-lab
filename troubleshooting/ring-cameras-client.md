# Three Ring cameras failing at a client's house

## What happened

A client had three Ring cameras that kept going offline. They were ready to replace them.

## How I tracked it down

Three cameras failing at once is rarely three bad cameras. I checked whether the problem followed the cameras or followed the network. The cameras themselves were fine. The trouble was the home's internet service, which wasn't holding a steady connection.

## The fix

I showed the client what I found and pointed them to their internet provider instead of new hardware. That saved them the cost of three cameras that didn't need replacing.

## What I took from it

When several devices fail together, look at what they share first, usually power or the network.
